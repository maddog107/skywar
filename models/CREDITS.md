## Conversion notes

- The CC0 Captain_Ahab_62 models (originally made for YSFlight) were exported from the `.blend` packs with Blender 5.2. Each aircraft was moved to the origin, scaled ×0.1 so units are meters, had its transforms and modifiers applied, and was exported with +Y up.
- `airliner.glb` was converted from `Jetliner.obj` with `obj2gltf`.
