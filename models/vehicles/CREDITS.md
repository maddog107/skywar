# Military vehicle models (`models/vehicles/`)

Rigged ground vehicles for the war layer, loaded by `src/vehicles.js` (see the header there for the rig API).
Unless listed otherwise below, every model here was built in Blender by the scripts in `tools/vehicles/`
(`vkit.py` kit, `chassis.py` shared chassis, one `<id>.py` per vehicle) and is **CC0 1.0** (public domain), like the
other SKYWAR-built models. Rebuild with `sh tools/vehicles/build.sh` (textures + every model).

The shared textures in `tex/` (camouflage paints, the detail palette, track links) are generated by
`tools/vehicles/textures.py` (CC0).
