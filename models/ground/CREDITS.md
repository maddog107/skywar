# Ground textures

Photo-scanned aerial ground textures (1k, diffuse + OpenGL normal), converted to WebP. Used for
the terrain's close-up detail layer (`GROUND` in `src/vegetation.js`: tiled at two scales, blended by
slope/height/cover, faded out with distance).
