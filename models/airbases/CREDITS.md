# Airbase models (`models/airbases/`)

Installations and base vehicles for the living airbases (`src/bases.js`, `src/basestructures.js`,
`src/baselife.js`, `src/coastal.js`), loaded by `src/airbasemodels.js`. Every model here was built in Blender by the
scripts in `tools/airbases/` (`akit.py` extends the vehicle kit `tools/vehicles/vkit.py` with the Concrete / Earth /
Steel structure materials; one `<id>.py` per model) and is **CC0 1.0** (public domain), like the other SKYWAR-built
models. Rebuild with `sh tools/airbases/build.sh` (textures and every model). Structures are authored about their
own origin (the game places them by it) with the door or front facing −Z; vehicles face −Z and are recentred.

The structure textures in `tex/` (`concrete.jpg`, `earth.jpg`, `steel.jpg`) are generated by
`tools/airbases/textures.py` (CC0). The vehicles also use the vehicle kit's shared textures in
`models/vehicles/tex/` (CC0).
