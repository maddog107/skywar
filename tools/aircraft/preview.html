Aircraft preview harness (does not start the game). Either use it from the console (await P.loadNew({...}); P.save(P.sheet(...), name), see preview.js) or pass a job in the URL hash:
preview.html#{"new":{"f22":{"file":"aircraft/f22.glb","rot":[0,0,0]}},"sheets":[{"title":"F-22","out":"cmp/f22.png","rows":[["old","f22"],["new","new_f22",{"markers":true}]]}]}
Results are POSTed as PNGs to P.UPLOAD (a tiny local upload server).
